Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Eastman Heavy Commercial Vehicle Batteries suitable for trucks?

Eastman HCV Batteries are designed to deliver dependable starting power and consistent performance for heavy commercial vehicles operating on highways, construction sites, and long-haul routes.

What is the capacity range of Eastman Heavy Commercial Vehicle Batteries?

Eastman HCV Batteries are available in capacities ranging from 130 Ah to 150 Ah, depending on the model.

What warranty is offered on Eastman Heavy Commercial Vehicle Batteries?

48 months* total coverage: 24 months replacement + 24 months pro-rata. Coverage varies by model and includes replacement and pro-rata periods. Confirm the applicable warranty and terms with an authorised Eastman dealer.

What is XUPER Alloy technology?

XUPER Alloy is Eastman's grid technology designed to maximise charge cycles, support longer battery life, and improve resistance to shocks and vibrations.

How do Eastman HCV Batteries handle road vibrations?

Their proprietary XUPER Alloy grid construction absorbs shocks and road vibrations, helping protect plate integrity during long-distance transportation and demanding driving conditions.

Are Eastman HCV Batteries suitable for extreme Indian weather?

Yes. They are engineered to perform in extreme Indian summers and high under-chassis temperatures, supporting reliable performance in demanding commercial environments.

How do Eastman HCV Batteries help reduce maintenance costs?

The built-in acid level indicator and special low-water-loss alloy help minimise maintenance requirements, service visits, and vehicle downtime.

Which Eastman Heavy Commercial Vehicle Battery models are available?

The catalogue lists two models: EM13048R — 130 Ah, EM15048R — 150 Ah
