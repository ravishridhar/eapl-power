Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Eastman Tractor Batteries suitable for agricultural use?

Eastman Tractor Batteries are designed to deliver reliable starting power and consistent performance in demanding agricultural conditions, including seasonal use, long idle periods, and challenging farm terrains.

What is the capacity range of Eastman Tractor Batteries?

Eastman Tractor Batteries are available in capacities ranging from 80 Ah to 100 Ah, depending on the model.

What warranty is offered on Eastman Tractor Batteries?

48 months* total coverage: 24 months replacement + 24 months pro-rata. Coverage varies by model and includes replacement and pro-rata periods. Confirm the applicable warranty and terms with an authorised Eastman dealer.

What is XUPER Alloy technology?

XUPER Alloy is Eastman's grid technology, designed to maximise charge cycles, support longer battery life, and improve resistance to shocks and vibrations.

How do Eastman Tractor Batteries handle vibrations on rough terrain?

Their proprietary XUPER Alloy grid construction absorbs shocks and field vibrations, helping protect plate integrity during agricultural operations.

Are Eastman Tractor Batteries suitable for extreme Indian weather?

Yes. They are engineered to perform in extreme Indian summers and high under-chassis temperatures, supporting dependable performance in demanding conditions.

Do Eastman Tractor Batteries require frequent maintenance?

They feature a built-in acid level indicator and special low-water-loss alloy to help reduce maintenance requirements, service visits, and downtime.

Which Eastman Tractor Battery models are available?

The catalogue lists three models: EM 80L48 R — 80 Ah, EM 90L48 L — 90 Ah, EM 100L48 L — 100 Ah
