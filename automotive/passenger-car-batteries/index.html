Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Eastman Passenger Car Batteries different?

Eastman Passenger Car Batteries feature XUPER Alloy grid technology, strong cranking performance, high vibration resistance and a low-maintenance design for dependable everyday driving.

What capacities are available in Eastman Passenger Car Batteries?

The catalogue lists battery capacities of 32 Ah, 35 Ah and 40 Ah across different models.

What is XUPER Alloy technology?

XUPER Alloy grid technology is designed to maximise charge cycles, improve vibration resistance and support longer battery life.

Are Eastman Passenger Car Batteries suitable for Indian weather conditions?

Yes. They are engineered to deliver reliable starting performance across varying weather conditions, including extreme Indian summers and high under-body temperatures.

Do Eastman Passenger Car Batteries require regular maintenance?

The batteries feature a built-in acid level indicator and a low-water-loss alloy to help reduce maintenance requirements. Follow the instructions for your specific model.

What warranty is available on Eastman Passenger Car Batteries?

Up to 72 months* total coverage: 36 months replacement + 36 months pro-rata. Coverage varies by model and includes replacement and pro-rata periods. Confirm the applicable warranty and terms with an authorised Eastman dealer.

How do I choose the right battery for my car?

Check your vehicle manufacturer's recommended battery capacity, dimensions and terminal layout. Consult an authorised Eastman dealer to confirm compatibility with your vehicle.
