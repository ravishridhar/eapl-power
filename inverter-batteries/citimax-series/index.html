FAQs

What is the CitiMax Series designed for?

Indian homes, specifically in cities, needing dependable, low-maintenance backup with more capacity than the rated label.

Which models are available?

Availability varies by capacity and model. Contact Eastman or an authorised dealer for the current CitiMax range.

What does ST and TT mean?

ST and TT identify the battery construction and model configuration. Refer to the selected model specification for details.

What is the warranty?

The CitiMax Series includes a 48-month warranty, subject to the terms for the selected model.

How many cycles will it last?

Cycle life depends on discharge depth, charging conditions, temperature and regular maintenance.

How should it be charged?

Use a compatible inverter or charger with the charging settings recommended for the selected CitiMax model.

What charging current does it need?

The required charging current depends on battery capacity. Follow the rating and charging guidance supplied with the model.

How much maintenance is involved?

Check electrolyte levels periodically, keep terminals clean and maintain adequate ventilation around the battery.

Is it safe to install indoors?

Install it in a dry, ventilated location and follow all clearance and safety instructions in the user manual.

How does it compare with the Smart Series?

CitiMax focuses on dependable city-home value and extra rated capacity, while Smart Series adds lower water loss and lower charging consumption across a broader range.
