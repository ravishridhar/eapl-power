FAQs

What makes the Smart Series different from the Regular Series?

It adds easier recovery after idle periods, lower recharging consumption and lower water loss, in a 100–230 Ah range.

What capacities are available?

The Smart Series is available across multiple capacities from 100 Ah to 400 Ah.

What warranty is offered?

Selected Smart Series models include warranty coverage of up to 60 months. Check the applicable model terms.

Can it be left unused for months?

Its recovery characteristics help it return to full capacity after an idle period when maintained and recharged correctly.

Is it safe to install indoors?

Low fume generation supports installation near living spaces. Always follow the ventilation and installation guidance.

How often does it need water topping?

Its low-water-loss design reduces topping frequency. Check levels at the intervals specified in the user manual.

Which inverter should it be paired with?

Pair it with an inverter whose charging profile and supported capacity match the selected battery model.

How do I choose the right capacity?

Choose capacity based on the connected load and required backup duration. Use the load calculator for an estimate.

What maintenance does it need?

Keep terminals clean, maintain the recommended water level and ensure the battery is charged with a compatible inverter.

When should it be replaced?

Persistent loss of backup time or failure to hold charge after correct maintenance can indicate replacement is due.
