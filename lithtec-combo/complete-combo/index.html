FAQs

What is the LithTec Combo?

It is a complete backup solution pairing an Eastman Home UPS with an Eastman Lead Acid Replacement Lithium Battery for reliable, efficient power.

What warranty does the LithTec Combo offer?

The system is backed by warranty coverage of up to 60 months, subject to the selected model terms.

Is the lithium battery maintenance-free?

Yes. The lithium battery requires no water topping and avoids the routine maintenance associated with lead-acid batteries.

Can I monitor the battery remotely?

Supported models can be monitored through the Eastman iConnect app for charge status, alerts and diagnostics.

Which UPS models are compatible with which LIB battery?

Use the combo chart above to match the Home UPS model and capacity with the recommended lithium battery.

Why choose lithium over a traditional lead-acid battery?

Lithium offers lower weight, faster charging, longer life and maintenance-free operation.

Does the combo protect against deep discharge and overcharging?

Yes. Smart battery management provides protection against deep discharge, overcharging and other operating faults.
