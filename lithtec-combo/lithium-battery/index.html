FAQs

What makes the Eastman lithium battery different from a regular lead-acid battery?

It is lighter, charges faster, needs no water topping and uses a smart BMS for protection and monitoring.

Is it compatible with my existing inverter/UPS setup?

Compatibility depends on inverter voltage and charging settings. Confirm the selected model requirements before installation.

What warranty does the LIB come with?

The lithium battery includes warranty coverage of up to 60 months, subject to the selected model terms.

How much does the battery weigh?

Weight varies by capacity and model. Refer to the selected product specification for the exact value.

What battery capacities and voltages are available?

The featured model is rated at 51.2 V and 150 Ah. Contact Eastman for current model availability.

What operating temperature range does the battery support?

Refer to the selected model specification and installation guide for its supported charging and discharge temperatures.
