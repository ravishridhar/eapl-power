FAQs

What is a tubular battery?

An inverter battery built with advanced tubular plate technology to deliver long backup and reliable performance.

What are the benefits of a tubular battery?

Tubular batteries provide long backup, strong deep-discharge recovery, reliable performance and a long service life.

How long does a tubular battery last?

Battery life depends on usage, charging conditions and maintenance. Follow the recommended care schedule for the longest service life.

How often should water be added?

Check electrolyte levels at the intervals stated in the user manual and top up only with approved distilled water when required.

Can it be used with any inverter?

Use an inverter whose charging profile and supported battery capacity match the selected Regular Series model.

Is it suitable for areas with frequent power cuts?

Yes. Its tubular construction and deep-discharge capability are designed for frequent power cuts and demanding use.

How long does it provide backup?

Backup time depends on battery capacity, charge level, connected load and battery condition.

What appliances can it run?

The connected inverter determines the supported appliances. Match the inverter and battery capacity to the total running and starting load.

How can I increase its life?

Keep terminals clean, maintain the recommended water level, avoid overloading and use a compatible inverter charging profile.

How do I know it needs replacement?

Persistent loss of backup time, slow charging or failure to hold charge after proper maintenance can indicate replacement is due.
