FAQs

What is the Eastman Wattrix Series home inverter?

The Wattrix Series is a square wave home UPS available from 700 VA to 1700 VA, built for compact homes that need efficient, dependable backup for lights, fans, TV and Wi-Fi.

Which inverter is best for a 1BHK or 2BHK home?

Choose the capacity for your connected appliances and their starting loads. Use our load calculator to estimate your requirements.

How long will the Wattrix inverter give backup?

Backup duration depends on battery capacity and condition, charge level, and connected load. Use the load calculator to estimate the battery capacity for your desired backup hours.

Can it handle coolers, pumps and mixers?

Peak Load Peak Output helps handle appliance start-up loads. Check the starting and running power of each appliance against your selected model’s capacity.

Will it work in areas with very low voltage?

The wide AC voltage range and Zero Volt Pickup support operation in low-voltage conditions. Check the operating limits for your model.

Is the changeover noticeable during a power cut?

Fast static changeover helps minimise interruptions when mains power fails. Actual performance depends on the connected equipment and load.

Does it protect the battery from damage?

Battery protection helps prevent deep discharge and overcharging, supporting battery performance and service life.

What is the warranty on the Eastman Wattrix Series?

The Wattrix Series has a 3-year (36-month*) warranty. Contact Eastman for the terms applicable to your model.
