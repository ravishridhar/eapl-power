FAQs

What is a pure sine wave inverter?

A pure sine wave inverter produces clean, grid-quality AC power identical to mains supply. The Sino Series delivers pure sine wave output, so sensitive electronics, LED lighting and modern appliances run exactly as they would on regular electricity.

Which inverter is best for home use?

Choose a model that supports your appliances and required backup duration. Use our load calculator to estimate your requirements.

Pure sine wave vs. square wave inverter — what is the difference?

Pure sine wave output closely follows mains electricity and supports sensitive electronics with quiet operation. Square wave output has a different waveform; check appliance compatibility before choosing.

What inverter VA capacity do I need for my home?

Add the power requirements of the appliances you want to run. Our load calculator helps estimate the required inverter capacity.

Can a home inverter run a refrigerator or AC?

Compatibility depends on the inverter model and the appliance’s running and starting load. Ask our team to confirm the right pairing.

Does an inverter switch over automatically during a power cut?

The Sino Series includes automatic static changeover when mains power fails.

How can I make my inverter battery last longer?

Follow the battery maintenance instructions, avoid overloading the inverter, and use a compatible battery and charging setup.

Is a home inverter safe for computers and electronics?

Pure sine wave output is suitable for sensitive electronics. Choose the correct capacity and follow the installation instructions.

Where can a pure sine wave inverter be used?

Applications include homes, small offices, retail stores, clinics, hotels and small schools, subject to the connected load.

What is the warranty on the Eastman Sino Series inverter?

The Sino Series is shown with a 5-year warranty. Contact Eastman for the terms applicable to your model.
