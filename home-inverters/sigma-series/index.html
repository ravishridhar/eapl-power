FAQs

What is the Eastman Sigma Series home inverter?

The Sigma Series is a square wave home UPS built for everyday Indian homes, available from 700 VA to 2000 VA. It delivers efficient backup with faster charging, wide voltage tolerance and enhanced protection.

Which inverter capacity should I choose for my home?

Choose a model based on the running and starting loads of the appliances you want to power. Use our load calculator to estimate your requirements.

Is a square wave inverter good for home use?

Square wave inverters provide economical backup for compatible everyday appliances. Check appliance requirements and choose a capacity that supports the connected load.

Does the Sigma Series protect against voltage fluctuation and lightning?

The Sigma Series supports a wide AC voltage range and includes lightning protection to help it operate in fluctuating supply conditions. Follow the installation and earthing requirements for your model.

Will the inverter start if the battery is deeply discharged?

Zero Volt Pickup supports recovery after deep discharge. Available backup still depends on the battery charge and condition.

Will my lights and Wi-Fi flicker when power fails?

Fast static changeover helps reduce interruptions when mains power fails. Equipment requirements and the connected load determine actual performance.

How does the Sigma Series extend battery life?

Battery protection helps prevent deep discharge and overcharging, supporting battery performance and service life.

What is the warranty on the Eastman Sigma Series?

The Sigma Series is shown with a 4-year (48-month*) warranty. Contact Eastman for the terms applicable to your model.
