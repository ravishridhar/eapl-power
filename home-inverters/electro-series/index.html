FAQs

What is a square wave inverter and where is it used?

A square wave inverter is an economical home UPS that powers fans, lights, TVs, coolers and other everyday appliances. The Eastman Electro Series uses Unity Power Factor technology for high efficiency and dependable full-load backup.

What is the difference between square wave and sine wave inverters?

Sine wave inverters produce a smooth waveform similar to mains power. Square wave inverters provide economical backup for compatible everyday appliances. Check your appliance requirements when choosing a system.

What capacity Electro inverter should I buy for my home?

Choose a capacity based on the running and starting loads of the appliances you want to power. Use our load calculator to estimate your requirements.

What is Peak Load Peak Output (PLPO)?

Peak Load Peak Output helps the inverter handle higher loads when multiple appliances start together, supporting dependable backup within its rated capacity.

Will the inverter work if the battery is deeply discharged?

Zero Volt Pickup supports recovery after deep discharge. Backup availability still depends on the battery charge and condition.

Does the changeover affect lights, fans or Wi-Fi?

The Electro Series uses fast static changeover to reduce interruptions when switching from mains power to backup.

How does the Electro Series protect my battery?

Battery protection helps prevent deep discharge and overcharging, supporting performance and battery life.

What warranty does the Eastman Electro Series carry?

The Electro Series carries a 5-year (60-month) warranty. Contact Eastman for the terms applicable to your model.
