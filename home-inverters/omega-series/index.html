FAQs

What is the Eastman Omega Series home inverter?

The Omega Series is a pure sine wave home UPS available from 1000 VA to 2000 VA, delivering grid-quality backup with smooth operation, faster charging and enhanced protection for modern homes.

Why choose a pure sine wave inverter?

Pure sine wave output provides smooth power for sensitive electronics and helps appliances operate quietly, without humming or performance issues.

Which Omega capacity is right for my home?

Choose a model based on the running and starting loads of the appliances you want to power. Use our load calculator to estimate your requirements.

Can the Omega Series run a refrigerator?

Compatibility depends on the refrigerator’s running and starting load, the inverter capacity and the battery setup. Contact our team to confirm the right model.

Will it work in areas with heavy voltage fluctuation?

The Omega Series supports a wide AC voltage range and includes lightning protection to help it operate in fluctuating supply conditions. Follow the installation and earthing requirements for your model.

Does the inverter work after deep battery discharge?

Zero Volt Pickup supports recovery after deep discharge. Available backup still depends on the battery charge and condition.

Will my computer or Wi-Fi restart during a power cut?

Fast static changeover helps reduce interruptions when mains power fails. Equipment requirements and the connected load determine actual performance.

What is the warranty on the Eastman Omega Series?

The Omega Series is shown with a 4-year (48-month*) warranty. Contact Eastman for the terms applicable to your model.
