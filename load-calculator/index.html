FAQs

Why is the Eastman Load Calculator intelligent?

The Eastman Load Calculator is a simple-to-use tool that helps you estimate your home's power requirement based on the appliances you want to run and your desired backup duration.

How do I use the Eastman Load Calculator?

Select your appliances, choose backup hours and preferences, then review the calculated recommendation.

What appliances can I add to the calculator?

You can include common lighting, fans, kitchen, entertainment, cooling and utility appliances.

How does the calculator determine my power requirement?

It totals the wattage of each selected appliance and adds a practical operating margin.

How much backup time can I select?

You can choose between 1 and 12 hours.

What is the difference between Sine Wave and Square Wave Home UPS?

Sine wave output is best for sensitive electronics, while square wave systems suit basic everyday loads.

Which battery type can I choose?

Choose between tubular and lithium batteries.

Should I choose a Tubular or Lithium battery?

Tubular batteries offer dependable value; lithium batteries are lighter, faster charging and maintenance-free.

What does “Your Load” mean?

Your Load is the combined wattage of the appliances you selected.

What does “VA Required” mean?

VA Required is the inverter capacity recommended after applying the operating margin.

Why is the number of backup hours important?

Longer backup duration requires greater battery storage capacity.
